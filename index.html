Notes
  • This simulator overstates revenue ~35% versus what the IRS actually collects, because it treats all AGI as ordinary income with no preferential cap-gains rates, itemized deductions, QBI, or credits. That's a documented modeling simplification.
  • Bracket counts are tax-filing units (158M returns, excluding 2.18M "no AGI" returns from Table 1.1 that fall below the log-scale floor), not households — joint filers count as one return, dependents may file separately, so the total exceeds the ~131M U.S. household count. All four custom curves (Linear, S-curve, Step, Exponential) interpolate in dollar space between the two thresholds, so they appear visually skewed on the log x-axis but are mathematically symmetric in income.
